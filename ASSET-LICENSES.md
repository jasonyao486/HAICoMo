## Characters: non-commercial use and distribution

Character artwork is derived from [ZipZipPipe](https://space.bilibili.com/4168597)'s works, including [大AI和小AI们](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1tE9XBbErS/) and [“亿万”身家的她们](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1bARXBYEZz/).

On 7 October 2026, the project owner confirmed that the permission obtained covers non-commercial use and distribution of the existing characters and adapted atlases in the public repository, free application packages and documentation screenshots. The project records this scope as `LicenseRef-Character-NonCommercial`; it is a description of the confirmed permission, **not** a claim that all characters are CC-licensed. Preserve attribution and modification notices. Commercial rights are not granted; obtain separate permission from the relevant rights holders. Private correspondence is not published.

Modifications include extraction of frames, cropping, background treatment, scaling, AI-assisted completion/redrawing, and animation-atlas production. These adaptations are not represented as independently originated character designs. Source videos and unrelated reference files are not distributed.
